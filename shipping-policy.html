Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Made to order

Every Pap's Supply Co. product is printed to order after you buy it — nothing sits in a warehouse. Production typically takes 2–7 business days before your order ships, and most orders leave production in 2–5 business days.

Delivery estimates (United States)

After production, US standard delivery typically takes 2–5 business days. In total, most US orders arrive within 5–10 business days of purchase. These are estimates, not guarantees — holidays and peak seasons can add time.

Shipping costs

Shipping is calculated and shown at checkout before you pay. US shipping starts at $5.29 for the first item; additional items in the same order ship at a reduced per-item rate.

Tracking

When your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email so you can follow it to your door.

Where we ship

We currently sell and ship within the United States.

Problems with delivery

If your order hasn't arrived within 30 days of purchase, contact us and we will make it right under our Returns & Refunds Policy. Double-check your shipping address at checkout — we can't be responsible for orders sent to an incorrectly entered address, but contact us and we'll help however we can.
