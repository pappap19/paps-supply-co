Privacy Policy — Pap's Supply Co.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Pap's Supply Co. ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it in connection with our Pinterest app integration and online storefronts.

Information We Collect

  • Pinterest account data: When you interact with our pins, Pinterest may share basic public profile information with us through their API.
  • Order information: When you purchase from our storefronts (Printify, Redbubble), those platforms process your order details under their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

  • To publish and manage our product pins on Pinterest.
  • To understand which designs resonate with our audience.
  • To improve our products and content.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not collect passwords or payment details through Pinterest.

Data Retention

We retain API-derived data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by Pinterest's developer terms.

Your Rights

You may revoke our app's access at any time via your Pinterest settings under Apps. Contact us with any privacy questions and we will respond promptly.
