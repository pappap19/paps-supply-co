Good to Know

What sizes do the stickers come in?

Every sticker on this page comes in 2", 3" and 4" die-cut sizes with a matte vinyl finish. The 2" fits a stocking or a phone case; the 4" reads across a room on a laptop or tumbler.

When will my order arrive?

Everything is printed to order when you buy it — allow 2–7 business days for production, plus shipping time. For Christmas delivery, order early in December rather than late.

Where can I stick them?

Laptops, journals, planners, tumblers, water bottles, gift wrap, and the tree itself if you use the ornament designs as tags. Anywhere that needs haunting.

Can I get several designs at once?

Yes — the Creepmas Classics 4-Pack puts four of the season's designs on one sheet for $16.99, and singles can be mixed freely in one order.
