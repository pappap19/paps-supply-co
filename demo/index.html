Pap's Supply Co Publisher

Pap's Supply Co publishes its product pins to Pinterest with this app. Connect your Pinterest account to authorize it — you will be sent to Pinterest to review and approve access (OAuth 2.0).

Requested permissions: read and write boards and pins, and read your account profile. No password is ever collected by this app.

One-time setup (app owner)

Paste the app secret key from the Pinterest developer portal. It is stored only in a short-lived, httpOnly cookie in this browser (15 minutes) so the sign-in below can complete — never saved to a server, database, or log.
